# Hive 0.1.3

Background music and sound effects for reviews and sessions, and fixes for stacks, sync, sign-in, sessions, and review.

## Music and sound effects

- Hive now plays short sounds for taps, locking in an answer, right and wrong answers, card flips, the next question, the last five seconds of a timer, time up, players joining, the quiz start, and the results.
- Music loops while you answer in a review, and in sessions. In Discussion sessions, only the Host's phone plays music. In Asynchronous sessions, each player's phone plays music in the lobby and while answering.
- Settings has a new Sound section, with separate switches for sound effects and music in solo reviews and in multiplayer sessions.
- Use the music button at the top of a review or a session to mute the music there only. Your Settings stay the same.
- Music stops when Hive goes to the background, pauses for calls, and does not interrupt music that another app is playing. With TalkBack on, music starts muted.

## Fixes

- Questions of different stacks stay separate on the phone, and imported stacks get new question IDs, so an import never changes another stack.
- Stacks with empty or duplicate question or choice IDs, or answers longer than 500 characters, are refused on the phone and on the server.
- Stack changes wait to sync when the app closes during an edit, and a new stack syncs only after you save it. Restoring a deleted stack before sync keeps it.
- Sign-in stays working when several requests need a new token at the same time. When the server ends your sign-in, the next sign-in continues to sync without copies.
- A session ends for everyone when its deadline passes, also in the lobby, and Host Setup says so. Timers start again after the server or the hosting phone restarts.
- Players can rejoin from the lobby without showing twice, and counts include only players who did not leave.
- Equal scores rank players with a known answer time first.
- History opens the results of past sessions, and Activity counts only reviews with answers and ended sessions.
- Reset data can reset this phone only, or this phone and your account, and says what failed.
- Continue with Google is locked when the server cannot sign you in.

## Install

Download `hive-0.1.3.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0 or newer phone. It updates an installed Hive 0.1.2 while keeping your local data. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.
