# Hive 0.1.0

The first release of Hive: make question stacks, review them on your own, and run live quizzes with friends, with or without internet.

## Make stacks

- Create stacks on your phone with no account or internet. Questions can be multiple choice, true or false, or identification, each with its own points and time limit.
- Edit in one place: one question card is open at a time, with Save and Cancel. Your changes are kept if the app closes, and Back asks before discarding them.
- Import stacks from Hive JSON or CSV files. Problems are listed by question or row before anything is saved.
- Copy a ready-made prompt to have an AI assistant turn your notes into a Hive file.
- For identification questions, choose how strict answers are: ignore capital letters, ignore symbols, and allow typos at Low, Medium or High.

## Review on your own

- Quiz Review shows if each answer is right, with the explanation.
- Flashcard Review lets you mark each card Got it or Review again.
- Reviews resume where you stopped, and the summary lets you retry only what you missed.
- Choose your answer colours: App honey, Green and red, or Blue and orange (easiest for colour-blind players). Right and wrong answers always show a check or a cross too.

## Host live quizzes

- Host a quiz from your phone with no internet. Nearby players on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot join with just the room code. The phone's address still works on networks that hide nearby rooms.
- Discussion rooms move together, question by question. Asynchronous rooms let each player go at their own pace until the deadline.
- Optional question timers, a quiz deadline, a speed bonus, and a tiebreak by answer time.
- Results show base points and the speed bonus apart.
- A player stays in one room at a time, and can leave and rejoin. Hosts can edit a room in the lobby, leave and come back, or end it for everyone.

## Sign in and sync

- Sign in with Google to host and join online rooms on the Hive server. Guests can still join online rooms by code, and everything else works without an account.
- Your stacks, finished reviews, and quiz results sync to every phone where you sign in with the same Google account. Changes made offline go up when the phone is back online.
- When two phones change the same stack, one keeps its version as a new stack with "(from this phone)" after the title, so no change is lost.
- The first sign-in asks whether to upload the stacks already on the phone or keep them on that phone only.
- Account shows whether sync is up to date, with Sync now, and a Quiz History of the quizzes you hosted and played.
- New stats: quizzes played, rooms hosted, a day streak, and your average score.
- Log Out asks whether to keep your synced stacks on the phone. Reset Data can reset this phone only, or your account too.

## New look

- The Wax and Graphite design: honey and graphite colours, bold condensed headings, and hexagon progress cells, in light and dark mode.
- The In flight bee as the app icon and splash screen.
- A Welcome screen, a new Home with Join, Host Quiz, Create Stack and Review, and an Account page with your real review history and stats.

## Known limits

- When the online server cannot be reached, online features are greyed out with the reason, and everything on your phone keeps working.
- A Google account must be added as a tester before it can sign in, until the app's Google sign-in is published.
- Hosting on a phone needs Wi-Fi or the phone's hotspot so that players can reach it.

## Install

Download `hive-0.1.0.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0 or newer phone. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.
