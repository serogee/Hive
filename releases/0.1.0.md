# Hive 0.1.0

The first release of Hive: make question stacks, review them on your own, and run live quizzes with friends, with or without internet.

## Make stacks

- Create stacks on your phone with no account or internet. Questions can be multiple choice, true or false, or identification, each with its own points and time limit.
- Edit in one place: one question card is open at a time, with Save and Cancel. Your changes are kept if the app closes, and Back asks before discarding them.
- Import stacks from Hive JSON or CSV files. Problems are listed by question or row before anything is saved.
- Copy a ready-made prompt to have an AI assistant turn your notes into a Hive file.
- For identification questions, choose how strict answers are: ignore capital letters, ignore symbols, and allow typos at Low, Medium or High.

## Review on your own

- Quiz Review shows if each answer is right, with the explanation.
- Flashcard Review lets you mark each card Got it or Review again.
- Reviews resume where you stopped, and the summary lets you retry only what you missed.
- Choose your answer colours: App honey, Green and red, or Blue and orange (easiest for colour-blind players). Right and wrong answers always show a check or a cross too.

## Host live quizzes

- Host a quiz from your phone with no internet. Nearby players on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot join with just the session code. The phone's address still works on networks that hide nearby sessions.
- Discussion sessions move together, question by question. Asynchronous sessions let each player go at their own pace until the deadline.
- Optional question timers, a quiz deadline, a speed bonus, and a tiebreak by answer time.
- Results show base points and the speed bonus apart.
- A player stays in one session at a time, and can leave and rejoin. Hosts can edit a session in the lobby, leave and come back, or end it for everyone.
- When the app opens, it lists the sessions you host that have been open for more than a day, so you can close them all or keep them open for another day.

## New look

- The Wax and Graphite design: honey colours, bold condensed headings, and hexagons with slightly rounded corners.
- Light mode keeps dark colours for text and icons only. Dark mode is pure black, which saves battery on AMOLED screens.
- The In flight bee as the app icon and splash screen.
- A Welcome screen, and a new Home with Join, Host Quiz, Create Stack and Review.
- A tab bar with Home, Stacks, Sessions and Account, where a honey hexagon slides to the tab you open. Screens change at once, with a very short fade.

## Sign in and sync

- Continue with Google to keep your stacks and history on your account. Everything also works without an account.
- Signed in, host and join online sessions over the internet with the session code, with no shared Wi-Fi needed.
- Stacks, finished reviews, and quiz results sync between your phones. Changes made offline wait and sync when you are back online.
- When two phones change the same stack, the other phone keeps its version as a new stack, with "(from this phone)" after the title.
- Sync runs by itself: after sign-in, when the app opens or comes back, and a few seconds after a change. The sync bar in Account says when it last synced, with a button to sync now.
- The first sign-in asks whether to upload the stacks already on your phone. Sign out asks whether to keep them on the phone.
- Account shows your day streak, the days of this week, your average score, and your totals under Activity, and your reviews and quizzes in one list under History.
- Settings, behind the gear button in Account, has the theme, answer colours, answering, the server, updates, and Reset data on this phone.
- Unsync and delete account deletes your account and everything the server keeps for it. Everything on this phone stays.
- When the server cannot be reached, online features are greyed out with the reason, and you stay signed in.

## Updates

- Check for updates in Settings finds newer versions of Hive on GitHub, downloads one, and checks that it is a Hive update signed with the same key before Android installs it.

## Known limits

- Hosting on a phone needs Wi-Fi or the phone's hotspot so that players can reach it.
- Until Hive's Google sign-in is published, only Google accounts added as testers can sign in.
- Check for updates can find new versions only when Hive's releases are public on GitHub.

## Install

Download `hive-0.1.0.apk` below and open it on an Android 8.0 or newer phone. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.
